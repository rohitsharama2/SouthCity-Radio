Radio South City

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

In short: you can listen without an account, and then your favorites and settings stay on your device. If you create an account, we keep your email address, your display name, and the stations and shows you follow, so they are there on every device you sign in to. We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use tracking analytics.

Who we are

Radio South City (“we”, “us”) runs the Radio South City Android app and website. For any privacy question or request, email rsouthcity@gmail.com.

Information we handle

Without an account

Your favorite stations, followed shows and reminders, recently played stations, the name you enter in your profile, and your theme and notification preferences are stored only on your device, in the app’s or browser’s own storage. We don’t receive them.

With an account (optional)

  • Email address, used to sign you in with a one-time link and to identify your account.
  • Display name, which you choose or which comes from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Favorite stations and followed shows, synced to your account.
  • Account role, which is “listener” unless the station makes you a staff member.
  • Sign-in records, such as when you signed in and from which IP address, kept by our authentication provider for security.

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. We don’t receive your Google password, contacts, or other Google data. Recently played stations, theme, and notification preference stay on each device even when you are signed in.

When you listen

Pressing play connects your device directly to that station’s streaming server, which receives your IP address and counts you as a current listener. The Radio South City live stream comes from our streaming provider; the sample stations in the app stream from SomaFM. We don’t keep a record of what you listen to on our servers.

Technical information

Like any website, our servers and service providers receive standard request details, such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the time of the request. Our hosting provider may keep these in short-term logs used to run and protect the service.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your library in sync across devices.
  • To send the sign-in emails you ask for.
  • To show what is playing on the live station and how many people are listening.
  • To let station staff manage the station, and to keep the service secure.

We don’t sell or rent your information, show advertising, or build advertising profiles.

Service providers

These companies process information for us, only to provide the service:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, and our database.
  • Render: hosting of our website and app server.
  • Our email delivery provider: sending sign-in emails.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose it, and the fonts used in the app.
  • Our streaming provider and SomaFM: audio streams and song information.
  • Unsplash and Pravatar: sample photos and profile pictures shown in the app.

These providers may store data in countries other than yours. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you ask us to delete it. On-device information stays until you clear it, sign out, or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Change your display name in your profile.
  • Use Profile → Privacy & your data → Clear my listening data to remove your favorites and followed shows from your account and from the device.
  • Sign out to remove your account’s copy from that device.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing rsouthcity@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days.

Android app permissions

The app uses internet access to play stations and sync your library, and a media playback notification so you can pause or stop the radio from the notification bar and lock screen. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

Radio South City is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to our server and to Supabase are encrypted (HTTPS), and database rules limit each account to its own information. The live audio stream currently uses an unencrypted connection; it carries audio and song information only, not your personal information. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us if you notice a problem.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
